# 🌿 Calmly

### AI-Assisted Anxiety Support & Remote Wellness Platform

> **Calmly is a personalized digital wellness platform that combines AI-driven behavioral insights, adaptive anxiety assessments, guided exercises, journaling, habit-based growth, and therapist supervision into one calm and privacy-conscious experience.**

Calmly is designed around a simple idea:

**Mental wellness support should not stop at recording how someone feels. It should help them understand their patterns, build healthy habits, and—when connected to a therapist—turn those patterns into meaningful, human-guided support.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Calmly exists to address three specific gaps:

### 1. The gap between therapy sessions
Traditional in-person therapy typically gives a patient one session a week. Anxiety, however, doesn't wait for a scheduled appointment — it shows up daily, in small moments that a once-a-week check-in can't capture. Calmly closes this gap by giving patients daily tools (journaling, quizzes, exercises) that keep a continuous record of how they're doing, so the support isn't limited to a single hour a week.

### 2. The stigma around seeking therapy in Pakistan
Mental health support is still a taboo topic in much of Pakistan, and many people avoid seeking help simply because of the social visibility of walking into a therapist's office or being seen discussing it. Calmly supports **remote, app-based therapy** — patients can connect with a verified therapist, journal, and receive feedback entirely through the app, lowering the social barrier to getting support.

### 3. Sustaining motivation over time
Mental wellness routines are easy to abandon once the initial motivation fades. Calmly addresses this by symbolizing the user's mental health and consistency through a **growing virtual plant** — as the user engages positively (journaling, exercises, quizzes), the plant grows. It's a gentle, non-clinical way of making small, consistent progress visible and motivating, without turning wellness into a competitive score.

# 🧠 Core Features

## 1. Personalized Weekly Anxiety Quiz

Calmly does not rely on the same static questionnaire every week.

The system uses previous quiz performance to personalize upcoming questions.

At onboarding, users can identify their anxiety type. If they are unsure, Calmly can guide them through questions to help determine the most relevant category.

The weekly assessment then adapts according to the user's previous responses.

```text
Previous Quiz Responses
          ↓
Personalization Logic
          ↓
Upcoming Questions
          ↓
Patient Responses
          ↓
Updated Behavioral Data
          ↓
Next Week's Personalization
```

This creates an assessment experience that evolves with the user rather than repeatedly asking the exact same questions.

# 📔 Intelligent Journaling

Calmly's journal combines structured and unstructured reflection.

Each entry contains:

* Mood
* Sleep quality
* Feelings
* Reflection
* Therapist-assigned tasks/todos

### AI is intentionally NOT used on every journal submission.

Instead, Calmly focuses AI analysis where it provides more value.

The system can compare the patient's current reflection and feelings against their recent journal history to identify signs of positive movement such as:

* increased self-awareness,
* recognition of anxiety triggers,
* healthier reflection,
* positive change in attitude,
* signs of personal growth.

This information contributes to Calmly's growth/scoring system.

### Why this approach?

Constantly generating AI feedback for every journal entry would add unnecessary noise and cost.

Calmly instead uses AI selectively, where **longitudinal comparison provides more meaningful information than isolated analysis.**

# 🌱 Calmly Growth System

Calmly turns consistent wellness behavior into a visual growth experience.

The patient's virtual plant progresses through multiple growth stages, and directly symbolizes the patient's mental health journey — a simple, ongoing visual signal of how they're doing over time.

Growth can be influenced by healthy engagement with Calmly, including:

* journal activity,
* weekly quiz progress,
* completing wellness exercises,
* other existing app-based progress signals.

The plant is not intended to represent medical recovery.

It represents something simpler:

> **Small consistent actions lead to visible progress.**

This provides a gentle form of motivation without turning mental wellness into a competitive score.

# 👩‍⚕️ Therapist Integration

Calmly extends beyond a standalone self-help application.

A patient can search for verified therapists available through the platform and send a connection request.

Once connected, the therapist can become part of the patient's Calmly experience.

### Therapist capabilities include:

* Patient management
* Patient activity monitoring
* Weekly AI-generated reports
* Therapist-assigned todos
* Therapist-created app-specific advice
* Weekly quiz review/customization
* Therapist-written feedback
* Patient progress observation

The therapist does **not** receive an uncontrolled stream of raw AI responses.

Instead, Calmly organizes relevant patient activity into structured reports.

# 🤖 AI-Generated Weekly Reports

One of Calmly's central AI features is the **Weekly Patient Report**.

Instead of asking AI to interpret everything independently, Calmly first calculates structured statistics from the database.

For example:

```text
Journal Activity
Mood Trends
Sleep Trends
Quiz Results
Exercise Activity
Behavioral Patterns
        │
        ▼
Structured Patient Data
        │
        ▼
AI Analysis
        │
        ▼
Weekly Patient Report
        │
        ▼
Therapist
```

This approach helps keep the AI grounded in actual application data.

The report focuses on **observations and trends**, rather than medical diagnosis.

### Example report areas

* Overall behavioral summary
* Mood and sleep trends
* Journal patterns
* Exercise engagement
* Quiz progress
* Areas that may require attention
* Observable changes over time

**Calmly does not position the AI as a therapist or diagnostic system.**

# 💬 Therapist Feedback

AI-generated reports and therapist feedback serve different purposes.

### AI Weekly Report

Used primarily by the therapist to understand patient activity.

### Therapist Feedback

Written personally by the therapist for the patient.

The therapist provides feedback through four structured sections:

1. **Overall Weekly Observation**
2. **Progress and Strength**
3. **Areas to Focus On**
4. **Feedback and Guidance**

This creates a human-in-the-loop model:

```text
Patient Activity
      ↓
AI analyzes patterns
      ↓
Therapist reviews insights
      ↓
Therapist adds human judgment
      ↓
Patient receives feedback
```

AI provides **information**.

The therapist provides **human guidance**.

# 📝 Therapist-Controlled Weekly Questions

Calmly combines AI personalization with human oversight.

Before a new week begins:

```text
AI plans personalized questions
             ↓
      Therapist reviews
          /       \
         /         \
    Changes?       No change
       ↓               ↓
Therapist version   AI version
       \               /
        └──────┬──────┘
               ↓
             Patient
```

If the therapist modifies the upcoming questions, the patient receives the therapist-adjusted version.

If the therapist does nothing, Calmly automatically uses the AI-planned questions.

This preserves automation **without removing professional oversight.**

# 🔐 Privacy & Security

Because Calmly handles personal wellness information, privacy is treated as a core engineering concern.

The platform uses:

* JWT-based authentication
* HTTP-only authentication cookies
* Password hashing with bcrypt
* Role-based access
* Server-side authorization
* Patient/therapist relationship checks
* Protected patient data access

Therapists should only be able to access patients who are actually connected to them.

Patients should only be able to access their own information.

Journal history is treated as particularly private and can be protected by additional password verification.

Calmly is structured around three different user roles.

| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 👤 Patient      | Journaling, quizzes, exercises, growth, therapist interaction |
| 👩‍⚕️ Therapist | Patient monitoring, reports, tasks, advice, feedback          |
| 🛡️ Admin       | Therapist verification and platform oversight                 |

### Therapist verification

Therapists submit professional documentation for verification.

The administrator reviews the submitted document before the therapist becomes available to patients through therapist search.

This creates a basic trust layer between patients and professionals on the platform.

# 🧠 Responsible AI Design

Calmly is designed with an important boundary:

> **AI assists with observation and personalization; it does not replace professional judgment.**

AI is used for:

* Personalizing weekly questions
* Comparing recent journal patterns
* Generating structured weekly reports
* Identifying observable themes and trends

AI is **not intended to**:

* Diagnose mental health conditions
* Prescribe medication
* Replace therapists
* Make clinical decisions
* Generate medical treatment plans

The therapist remains the human decision-maker in the therapist-assisted workflow.
